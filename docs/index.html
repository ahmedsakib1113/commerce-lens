EVIDENCE → ACTION

Three questions for the business.

Observations are calculated from the same scope as the figures above. Recommendations are proposed next steps.

OBSERVATION 01

Protect the leading category

Health Beauty contributes 9.3% of delivered merchandise sales.

Suggested action. Review availability and supplier coverage before expanding promotion. Sales alone do not establish margin.

OBSERVATION 02

Investigate delivery reliability

AL has 21.5% late deliveries across 396 eligible orders (minimum 100 per state).

Suggested action. Audit carrier routes and promised delivery windows in this state before changing service commitments.

OBSERVATION 03

Connect service to customer experience

Late orders average 2.27/5 from 6,378 reviews; on-time orders average 4.29/5 from 89,182.

Suggested action. Investigate the review gap and test proactive delivery updates. This observed association does not prove causation.

Built to be checked.

Python cleans the public source into a normalized SQLite warehouse. SQL creates one order fact per order and a separate order-category mart. Integer cents preserve monetary totals; eight automated quality checks reconcile the result. The Streamlit app provides date and state filters, category search, delivery sample thresholds, and CSV exports.

Source coverage: 2016-09-04–2018-10-17; 99,441 source orders and 112,650 line items. This report uses 1 Jan 2017–31 Aug 2018 to avoid sparse launch/closing months. All figures are historical.

Limits: Sales exclude freight and are not profit. Missing delivery dates are excluded from lateness. Review comparisons show association, not causation. Costs, refunds, ad spend, and stock history are unavailable. Repeat purchases within a window are not lifetime retention.
